Privacy Policy

How we handle the personal data of guests and visitors to this website.

Who is responsible

Villa24 Vlorë, Rr. 7 Nëntori, Vlorë 9401, Albania, is the data controller for the personal data described here.

For anything concerning your data, write to villa24vlore@gmail.com or call +355 69 204 9179.

This website does not collect anything from you

There is no form on this site that sends data to us. The booking bar on the home page does not submit anything: it only assembles a message and opens your own email client or WhatsApp, so you can see it and decide whether to send it. Until you choose to send that message, we receive nothing.

What we do receive, and when

  • When you contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp: your name, the address or number you write from, the dates and party you ask about, and whatever else you choose to tell us.
  • When you stay with us: the details needed to arrange and invoice the stay, and the identity details that Albanian law requires accommodation providers to record for their guests.
  • Server logs: this website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, it records technical request data including IP addresses. We do not have access to these logs and we do not analyse them. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • If you book through Booking.com: your reservation data reaches us through that platform, which is a separate controller with its own privacy policy.

Why we use it, and on what basis

To answer your enquiry and prepare a stay
Steps taken at your request before a contract, and performance of the accommodation contract itself.
To meet legal obligations
Guest records, tax and accounting duties that apply to accommodation providers in Albania.
To keep in touch about a booking you have made
Our legitimate interest in running the property and dealing with any question or dispute about a stay.

We do not use your data for marketing, we do not build profiles, and we make no automated decisions about you.

Who else sees it

We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing. It may be seen by our accountant, by the authorities where the law requires guest or tax reporting, and by the email or messaging provider that carries our correspondence. Nothing more.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not turn into a stay are kept only while the conversation is useful, and then deleted. Records connected to an actual stay are kept for as long as tax and accounting rules require, and then deleted.

Your rights

Under Albanian data protection law — and under the GDPR if you write to us from the European Union — you can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or receive it in a portable form. Write to villa24vlore@gmail.com and we will answer within a month.

If you think we have handled your data badly, you can complain to the Albanian Information and Data Protection Commissioner (idp.al), or to the supervisory authority of the EU country you live in.

Children

This website is not aimed at children. Where children stay with us, we only handle the details their parent or guardian gives us as part of the booking.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date at the top.

Cookie Policy

What this website stores on your device.

Nothing. This site sets no cookies of any kind — not even technical or preference cookies — and stores nothing in your browser's local storage or session storage.

Why there is no cookie banner

A consent banner exists to ask permission for cookies and similar tracking. There is nothing here to ask permission for, so there is no banner. The site is a single static page: the images, the text and the small amount of JavaScript that switches language and opens the photo gallery all come from this same website, and none of them writes to your device.

What we do not use

  • No analytics or statistics tools of any kind.
  • No advertising, remarketing or conversion pixels.
  • No social network buttons, widgets or embedded posts.
  • No embedded maps, videos or chat widgets.
  • No external fonts, scripts or stylesheets — the page uses the fonts already on your device.

Links that take you elsewhere

The page links out to Booking.com, Instagram, WhatsApp and Google Maps. Those are ordinary links: nothing is loaded from those services while you are on our page, and they learn nothing about your visit unless you click through. Once you do, you are on their site and their own cookie and privacy policies apply — not this one.

Server logs

Our host records technical request data, as described in the Privacy Policy. Those are server-side logs, not cookies, and they do not involve anything being stored on your device.
